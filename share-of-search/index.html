Live connection Not connected

Google doesn’t offer an open Trends API. The official one is in closed alpha, and Google Trends blocks requests made directly from a web page. A small, free connector running on Cloudflare fixes that. Once it is set up, Fetch live pulls the comparison straight into the tracker.

  1. Download worker.js.
  2. Sign up for a free Cloudflare account. Go to Workers & Pages → Create → Worker, paste in the file and deploy it.
  3. Recommended: sign up for a SerpApi key (the free plan covers regular tracking) and add it to the worker as a secret named SERPAPI_KEY. Without the key, the worker asks Google Trends directly, and Google sometimes refuses those requests.
  4. Paste the worker’s address below. It is only saved in this browser.

    Method

    A fast, cheap early warning for market share

    People search for the brands they are thinking of buying. So a brand’s share of all branded searches in its category is a practical measure of how much of the category’s demand it is winning.

    What it measures

    Share of the conversation people start

    Share of search is a brand’s search volume divided by the total for every brand in the set. The tracker does this for every week or month, so the bands always add up to 100%.

    Why it matters

    It tends to move before sales do

    In Les Binet’s work for the IPA, share of search tracked market share in cars, energy and mobile phones, and often moved ahead of it, by up to a year for cars. It shows correlation, not causation, and price still shapes conversion.

    How to read it

    Read the trend, not the week

    Search is seasonal and responds to news. Use a 6–12 month rolling average to see whether a brand is really gaining. Then switch to Interest to check whether the whole category is growing or shrinking.

    Good practice

    • Download all brands together.Google Trends scales every term in a comparison against the same peak. That makes the brands in one download comparable with each other. Numbers from separate downloads can’t be combined.
    • Choose the search terms carefully.Use the name people actually type. For ambiguous names like Apple, Shell or Orange, pick a category in Google Trends or use its topic suggestion, and keep the same setup every time you refresh.
    • Small brands are approximate.Google shows very low values as “<1”. The tracker counts them as 0.5, so shares below a few percent are rough.
    • Expect small wobbles.Google Trends uses a sample of searches, so a new download can differ by a point or two. The long-term trend holds up. A single week doesn’t tell you much.
